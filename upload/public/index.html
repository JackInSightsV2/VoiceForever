Share your Capture

VoiceForever records dialogue it has no voice for yet. Upload your saved addon data and new lines can be voiced in a future Voice Pack.

Where is the file?

  1. In game, type /vf export to see how many records you have, then /reload or log out so the game writes them to disk.
  2. Open your World of Warcraft folder, then the game folder you play from, then WTF/Account/<ACCOUNT>/SavedVariables/VoiceForever.lua Windows: usually under C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\. macOS: usually under /Applications/World of Warcraft/. <ACCOUNT> is your account name in capitals.
  3. Upload VoiceForever.lua itself, not VoiceForever.lua.bak or a file from another addon.

The file holds the NPC dialogue you saw, the NPCs' names and where you met them, and your client's language. Your character's name is in the dialogue text as the game showed it. Uploads are reviewed before anything is voiced; nothing is published automatically.
